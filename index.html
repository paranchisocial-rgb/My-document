About

Paranchi's Documents

A useful document app to keep your important PDFs and images organised, private and always with you.

Features
Folders. Sort documents into folders with your own icon and colour.
PDF and image viewer. Open documents right inside the app.
Works offline. Everything stays on your device. Nothing is uploaded.
Backup. Export folders to a ZIP and import them back from Settings.
